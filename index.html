Frequently Asked Questions

Everything You Need To Know About Trading With Us

Got questions about gold testing, minimum quantities, or WhatsApp orders? Here are the most common inquiries.

How does WhatsApp ordering work at T.J Concepts?
When you select your desired service or calculate a quote on our site, clicking "Send to WhatsApp" pre-formats all your specifications (weight, purity karat, preferred settlement) into a concise message. Our chief bullion officer responds immediately to confirm current live pricing and arrange delivery or assaying.
What is the minimum quantity of gold I can buy or sell?
For retail customers, we buy and sell from as little as 10 grams (or 1 Troy Ounce). For refining batches, our induction smelters efficiently process batches starting at 100 grams up to industrial lots exceeding 50 kilograms.
How do you determine the purity and valuation of scrap gold?
We use laboratory-grade Thermo Scientific X-ray Fluorescence (XRF) spectrometers to determine elemental composition to 0.01% accuracy without damaging your item. For larger melted bars, we conduct certified fire assaying (cupellation). Payouts are benchmarked directly against the international LBMA spot price.
Do you provide export permits and international delivery?
Yes. T.J Concepts is fully licensed to prepare all government royalties, mineral export documentation, customs clearance, and insured armored freight (Brink's, Malca-Amit, Ferrari) to major gold hubs including Dubai (DMCC), Zurich, London, and Antwerp.
What documents are required to trade or sell gold?
In compliance with Anti-Money Laundering (AML) and Know Your Customer (KYC) regulations, individual sellers need a valid government ID or Passport. Mining cooperatives and corporate entities must provide mineral dealer licenses, origin declarations, and company registration certificates.
How long does the gold refining process take?
Standard induction smelting and XRF assay takes under 45 minutes for batches up to 5kg. Full chemical Aqua Regia refining to 999.9 fine gold is typically completed same-day or within 24 hours depending on batch size.
